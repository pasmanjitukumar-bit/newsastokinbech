# SastoKinBech demo

A responsive Nepali storefront built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve this directory with `python -m http.server 3000`.

Includes product search, category filters, cart quantities and removal, browser-local cart persistence, a demo sale countdown, and mobile layouts. Product imagery is loaded from Unsplash; fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and require internet access.

Click a product image or name to open its details at `/?product=1` (IDs 1–9). Product 9 is the user-provided white off-shoulder dress, with local images, description, a white color option and size to be confirmed by phone. Its offer price is NPR 1,999 (original NPR 2,999). Product 2, the denim jacket, costs NPR 2,999 (original NPR 3,999). Other products retain sample content. The home page's product-page button opens the first product. Detail pages include photo views, category-appropriate sizes, sample color choices, quantity/stock checks, variant-aware local cart, delivery estimates, specifications, size charts, return terms and a reviews placeholder. Buy now opens the demo checkout form. Call/WhatsApp remain disabled until genuine seller contact details are supplied.

To serve this experience at `https://product.sastokinbech.com/`, deploy all HTML/CSS/JS files to a static host and configure that subdomain in the hosting provider and DNS. The corresponding product URL will be `https://product.sastokinbech.com/?product=1`. No DNS or remote hosting changes have been made. Photos are sample images; thumbnails are zoom views of the same photo, not additional actual product photos. The UI labels seller, stock, delivery and policy content as demo data.

Checkout is available at `/?checkout=1` from the cart or Buy now. It validates customer/address fields and Nepal mobile numbers (including Nepali digits), preserves selected product variants, displays delivery estimates, and calculates shipping: NPR 100 inside Kathmandu Valley, NPR 150 outside, or free on merchandise totals of NPR 2,000 or more. Empty carts show a shopping link. Submitting displays a clearly labeled demo confirmation and clears the cart. Personal form details are not sent to a server or saved in browser storage. Cash on Delivery is a demo selection; online payment gateways are not connected.

After confirmation, checkout opens a separate `/?thankyou=DEMO-…` page with the reference, timestamp, selected items, totals, demo delivery estimate and shopping links. Refresh restores the latest receipt within the same tab through sessionStorage. Only non-contact order-summary data is stored; customer name, phone and address are excluded. Missing or mismatched receipt references show an unavailable state. No real order is sent to a seller.

All products, prices, reviews, delivery claims, return claims, contact details and discounts are sample content. This demo does not authenticate users, place real orders or process payments. Replace sample information and connect a backend before using as a live store.
